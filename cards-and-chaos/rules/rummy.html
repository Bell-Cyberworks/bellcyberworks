How to Play: Rummy

Card game · 2–6 players · Everyone · $2.99

Goal

Meld your hand and lay off on the table until you're the first to empty your hand. Play a series of rounds until one player's score reaches 100.

Setup

A standard 52-card deck is shuffled. With 2 players, each is dealt 10 cards; with 3–6 players, each is dealt 7. The next card starts the discard pile; the rest becomes the stock. The dealer rotates to the next seat every round.

Melds

A meld is either 3 or 4 of the same rank, or a run of 3 or more consecutive cards in the same suit. Aces are low only.

Playing a turn

  1. Draw one card, from the stock or the top of the discard pile — there's no restriction on taking the discard here, unlike some rummy variants.
  2. Any number of times, in any order: meld a new set or run from your hand onto the table, or lay off a card from your hand onto any meld already on the table — yours or another player's.
  3. Discard one card to end your turn — unless melding or laying off just emptied your hand, in which case you've gone out and no discard is needed.

Going out and scoring

The moment a player empties their hand, the round ends immediately. They score the total point value of every card left in every other player's hand: Aces count 1, face cards count 10, everything else is face value. Whatever's still in a losing hand is simply their deadwood — nothing hidden or optimized for you.

Running out of stock

If the stock ever runs out, it's reshuffled from the discard pile (keeping the current top card in place) and play continues — the round never stalls out.

Winning

Every seated player plays every round — nobody is ever eliminated. Rounds keep being dealt until a player's cumulative score reaches 100; they win the match.
