How to Play: Ship-Captain-Crew

Dice game · 2–8 players · Everyone · $2.99

Goal

The classic pub dice game. Roll for a 6, a 5, then a 4 — ship, captain, crew — then bank whatever's left as your cargo. Highest total after 5 rounds wins.

Setup

5 dice, no cards, nothing hidden — every roll is visible to the whole table. The game runs for a fixed 5 rounds, and every seated player gets exactly one turn per round, starting from the lowest seat number.

Playing a turn

  1. Roll all 5 dice.
  2. You need a 6 (the "Ship") before anything else counts. Once you have a Ship, you need a 5 (the "Captain"). Once you have a Ship and Captain, you need a 4 (the "Crew"). Each roll locks at most one qualifying die, in that strict order — rolling extra 6s, 5s, or 4s before you're ready for them doesn't do anything extra.
  3. You get up to 3 rolls total to complete Ship, Captain, and Crew, rerolling only your unlocked dice each time.
  4. Once all three are locked with a roll still remaining, you can either bank the sum of your two remaining "cargo" dice as your score for the turn, or reroll just those two cargo dice for a shot at a higher total — and keep doing that for as many rolls as you have left of your 3 total, if you lock all three early.

Busting

If you haven't locked Ship, Captain, and Crew by the end of your 3rd roll, your turn scores 0 — no cargo, no partial credit.

Scoring

A completed turn scores the total of your two cargo dice — anywhere from 2 to 12. Your score across all 5 rounds adds up to your running total.

Winning

After all 5 rounds are complete, whoever has the highest total wins. If two or more players are tied for the highest total, the player in the lowest seat number wins.
