How to Play: Pass the Trash

Card game · 3–8 players · Everyone · $2.99

Goal

Don't be left holding the lowest card. Be the last player still holding tokens to win.

Setup

Every player starts with 3 tokens. One seat is the dealer for the whole game (starting with whoever hosts). Each round, every player still in the game is dealt one card, face down and visible only to them.

Playing a round

  1. Starting with the player to the dealer's left and going in turn order, each player decides: keep your card, or pass it — forcing a swap with the next player in turn order (whether they like it or not).
  2. When it's the dealer's turn, instead of trading with another player, they trade with the deck.
  3. Once everyone has acted, all hands are revealed.
  4. Whoever is holding the lowest card loses a token. If there's a tie for lowest, everyone tied loses a token.
  5. Cards rank 2 (lowest) through Ace (highest).

The Ace exception

If an Ace is dealt to anyone at the start of a round, that round skips trading entirely — everyone is stuck with whatever card they were dealt, and hands go straight to reveal.

Elimination and winning

Once a player runs out of tokens, they're eliminated and sit out for the rest of the game (if the dealer is eliminated, the dealer role passes to the next active player). Play continues, dealing a new round to whoever's left, until only one player still has tokens — they win.
