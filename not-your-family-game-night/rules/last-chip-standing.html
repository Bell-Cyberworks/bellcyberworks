How to Play: Last Chip Standing

Dice game · 3–8 players · Everyone · $2.99

Goal

Be the last player holding any tokens. Whoever's left standing wins the whole pot.

Setup

Every player starts with 3 tokens. Turns go around the table in seat order.

Taking your turn

On your turn, you roll one die for each token you're holding — up to a maximum of 3 dice, even if you have more than 3 tokens. If you have no tokens, your turn is skipped automatically.

RollEffect
1, 2, or 3Nothing — keep that token
4That token goes into the center pot
5That token passes to the player on your left
6That token passes to the player on your right

Winning

Once you're the only player left holding any tokens at all, the game watches your very next roll closely: if none of your dice come up 4, 5, or 6 (meaning you don't lose or pass away a single token), you win the entire pot immediately and the game ends. If even one die moves a token away, play just continues as normal.

If everyone runs out at once

It's possible for every last token to end up in the center pot with nobody holding any at all. If that happens, everyone is dealt back in with 3 fresh tokens each, and the pot carries over — nothing is lost, the game just keeps going.
