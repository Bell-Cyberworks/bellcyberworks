How to Play: Family Trivia/Prompt Game

Custom · 3–10 players · Everyone · $2.99

Goal

Answer prompts about yourself, then guess which of your friends or family wrote which answer. Score the most correct guesses over 5 rounds to win.

Playing a round

  1. Everyone sees the same prompt (for example, "What's your go-to comfort food?") and privately submits their own answer.
  2. Once everyone has answered — or the host chooses to reveal — every answer is shown to the whole table, without saying who wrote it.
  3. For each answer that isn't your own, guess who you think wrote it by tapping their name.
  4. You're told right away whether each guess was correct.

The game runs for 5 rounds, each with a new prompt, then ends automatically.

Scoring

Every correct guess (matching an answer to the person who wrote it) is worth 1 point. Whoever has the most correct guesses after all 5 rounds wins.

Good to know

If a kid account is part of your session, answers are limited to a preset list of family-friendly responses instead of open text, to keep things appropriate for everyone at the table.
