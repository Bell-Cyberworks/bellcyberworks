How to Play: Trick-Taking Classic

Card game · Exactly 4 players · Everyone · $2.99

Goal

Unlike most trick-taking games, you're trying to avoid points here. Lowest total penalty score after all 13 tricks wins.

Setup

A standard 52-card deck is shuffled and dealt out evenly — 13 cards to each of the 4 players. There's no trump suit.

Playing a trick

  1. The lead player plays any card from their hand, setting the suit for the trick.
  2. Going around the table, each other player must follow suit (play a card of the same suit) if they have one. If you don't have any card in the led suit, you may play any card.
  3. Once all 4 players have played, whoever played the highest card of the led suit wins the trick (cards of other suits can never win a trick, no matter how high).
  4. The trick winner leads the next trick. This repeats until all 13 tricks have been played.

Scoring — penalty points

Whoever wins a trick collects any penalty points in it:

  • Every Diamond in the trick is worth 1 penalty point.
  • The King of Clubs is worth 13 penalty points.
  • All other cards are worth 0.

After all 13 tricks are played, whoever has the lowest total penalty points wins the hand.
