How to Play: Push-Your-Luck Dice

Dice game · 2–6 players · Everyone · $2.99

Goal

Be the first player to bank 5,000 points across your turns.

Turn order

Players take turns one at a time, in seat order. On your turn, you roll all 6 dice and decide how far to push your luck.

Scoring a roll

RollPoints
Single 1100
Single 550
Three of a kindFace value × 100 (three 1s = 1,000)
Four of a kindDouble the three-of-a-kind value
Five of a kindTriple the three-of-a-kind value
Six of a kindQuadruple the three-of-a-kind value

Only 1s, 5s, and three-or-more-of-a-kind ever score. Any other dice in the roll don't count for anything.

Playing your turn

  1. Roll all 6 dice. If nothing scores at all, you bust — your turn ends immediately and you bank nothing for it, even if you'd already banked points earlier in this same turn.
  2. If something scores, you can either bank your turn's points and end your turn safely, or reroll the dice that didn't score to try for more.
  3. If every one of your 6 dice ends up scoring (across one or more rolls), you get all 6 back to roll again — "hot dice" — and can keep pushing your luck with a fresh set.
  4. Every reroll risks busting and losing everything you've built up this turn. Points from previous turns you've already banked are always safe.

Winning

The moment a player banks a turn that brings their running total to 5,000 or more, the game ends and that player wins.
