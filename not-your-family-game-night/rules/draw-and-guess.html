How to Play: Draw & Guess

Picture game · 3–8 players · Everyone · Free

Goal

Guess what's being drawn faster than anyone else. Whoever guesses correctly the most times across all the rounds you play wins.

Setup

The host taps "Next round" to start. One player is picked as the drawer for the round (this rotates automatically to a new player each round), and they're given a secret word to sketch.

Playing a round

  1. The drawer sees their secret word and starts sketching on the shared canvas — everyone else watches it appear live.
  2. Everyone who isn't drawing types guesses as the drawing takes shape.
  3. A correct guess earns that player 1 point for the round. You'll see how many people have guessed correctly so far, but not what anyone actually typed.
  4. The host can start the next round whenever they're ready — there's no fixed number of rounds, so play as many as the group wants.

Scoring

Each correct guess is worth 1 point. The drawer doesn't score on their own round. Whoever has the most correct guesses when the group stops playing wins.

Good to know

  • Words to guess always come from a fixed, family-friendly word list — nobody submits their own words.
  • What you type as a guess is never shown to other players or saved — only whether you got it right.
