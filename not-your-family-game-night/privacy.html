Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026 · Not Your Family Game Night is operated by Bell Cyberworks LLC ("we", "us"). Questions? Email support@bellcyberworks.com.

What we collect

  • Account info: the email address and display name you sign up with.
  • Game data: the sessions you host or join, invite codes, who's seated where, and the live game state for whatever you're playing (cards, dice rolls, drawings, answers, scores) so the game can run in real time for everyone in the session.
  • Scorecard history: which sessions you've completed and who won, so we can show you your family scorecard.
  • Purchase records: which games you've unlocked and any unredeemed Game Credits, handled through Apple's App Store or Google Play (see "In-app purchases" below).
  • Basic technical logs: standard request logs kept by our hosting provider to keep the service running and secure.

How we use it

  • To let you sign in and host or join live multiplayer game sessions.
  • To sync game state in real time between everyone in a session.
  • To track which games you own and unlock them accordingly.
  • To show you your family scorecard.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your game data for advertising.

User-generated content

Some games involve content you create during play - typed guesses, prompt answers, or freehand drawings. Depending on the game, this content is persisted for the duration of the game and shown, or broadcast in real time, to the other players in your session - for example, a Draw & Guess sketch is visible to everyone in the session as you create it. We don't currently screen this content with automated moderation tools; some games instead limit what can be typed to a fixed set of kid-safe options when a kid profile is in the session (see "Children" below). None of this content is used for advertising. If another player shares something inappropriate, you can report it in-app where available (for example, Draw & Guess's "Report this drawing") or email support@bellcyberworks.com.

Who processes your data for us

We use trusted infrastructure providers to run the app, including our cloud hosting, database, and authentication provider (Supabase).

In-app purchases

Games are sold as one-time in-app purchases through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment itself is handled by Apple or Google - we never see your card details.

We use RevenueCat to manage purchase entitlements and to tell our backend when a purchase happens. To do this, RevenueCat receives a pseudonymous app user id we generate for your account, along with purchase metadata from the store (product id, transaction id, purchase timestamp, platform, and similar fields). RevenueCat does not receive your email address, game data, or scorecard history from us. Their handling is described in the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. You can delete your own account at any time from Account settings in the app. Deleting your account removes your profile, purchase records, and your participation in any game sessions - including hands you played, turns you took, and wins recorded on the scorecard - even in sessions shared with other players (their own records stay intact).

Account deletion is blocked while a game you're in is actively being played, so other players aren't left stuck mid-game - finish the game first, then delete your account. Sessions still waiting in the lobby don't block deletion.

Your privacy rights (EEA, UK, and similar laws)

If data protection laws like the EU or UK GDPR apply to you, our lawful basis for the processing described above is: performance of our contract with you (running the app and your account), and our legitimate interests (keeping the service secure and working). Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Delete your data, or restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority, in addition to contacting us directly.

To exercise any of these rights, email support@bellcyberworks.com.

California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)

We do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. The categories of personal information we collect are described above under "What we collect." If you're a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, request its deletion, and not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. Contact support@bellcyberworks.com to make a request.

Children

Not Your Family Game Night is a family game app, and it supports parent-managed profiles for kids under 13 so a whole family can play together. We have actual knowledge that some accounts are used by children under 13, and this section explains how that works and what it means under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

How a kid profile is created. A kid profile can only be created by a parent from inside the parent's own signed-in adult account - a child never creates their own under-13 profile, signs up independently, or provides us with information directly. The parent enters the child's display name, an optional avatar, and date of birth on the child's behalf; the date of birth is used only to apply the age-appropriate settings described below and is never shown to other players. Before a parent can create a kid profile, they must first have their own account with a confirmed email address; a kid profile can then only be added to a game session by that same parent, from their own signed-in device.

What a kid profile can do, and what's restricted. A kid profile has no email, password, or independent login of its own - it can only be used from the parent's own signed-in device, or added to a session by the parent from the Lobby screen. A kid profile cannot make in-app purchases; only the parent can redeem a Game Credit they purchased on the kid's behalf. Games rated above "Everyone" can only be joined by a kid profile when their own parent is hosting. Where a game would otherwise involve open-ended free text, a kid profile is limited to a fixed set of kid-safe answers instead. For Draw & Guess specifically, a kid profile is not assigned to draw as long as at least one non-kid player is in the session (in the rare case where every player in a session is a kid, a kid may need to draw since there's no other player available) - and a kid may still see what another player draws, which is not currently screened by automated content-safety tools; see "User-generated content" above for how to report anything concerning.

What we collect and how it's used. For a kid profile, we collect only the display name, avatar, and date of birth the parent enters, plus the same gameplay and session data described in "What we collect" above for whichever games the kid plays. We don't collect this information directly from the child, and we don't use it for advertising. A kid's display name, avatar, and in-game content (like a Draw & Guess sketch or Family Trivia answer) are visible to the other players in the same game session, the same as for any player - see "User-generated content" above. Beyond that in-session visibility, we don't share it with anyone other than the infrastructure providers described in "Who processes your data for us."

Reviewing and deleting a kid's data. A parent can review the kid profiles they manage - display name and age - directly in Account settings at any time, and can request the exact information on file, including date of birth, by emailing us. A parent can delete their own account at any time from Account settings, which also deletes every kid profile they manage. To delete just one kid's profile without deleting your own account, review what's on file for a kid in more detail, or if you believe a child has provided us with personal information some other way, email support@bellcyberworks.com and we will help.

Teens 13 and older can sign up for their own independent account (not a parent-managed profile) using a link code their parent generates. That account is treated the same as any other account under this policy, since COPPA's requirements for children under 13 don't apply to it.

Wherever your data is processed, we require our providers to protect it consistent with this policy and, where applicable law requires it, through appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.
